About the Author

M y name is Raktimjyoti Sarma. In 2026, I appeared CBSE Class 12 board examinations alone from my school. Not alone in the sense of without support — alone in the sense that there was no one else in the batch. My school had never had a 12th grade before. I was the first student to get that far, and the only one.

Most of my school life had classmates, noise, and the usual reference points. My last two years did not. In 11th and 12th grade, I was the only student in my batch, and I was the first batch — no cohort to compare notes with, no senior who had been through it before. I am genuinely fine with that. I did not mind. Some people need the room to be full before they can work. I am not one of them.

What I do with that solitude is build things and think about how they fit together. When I built my first real project, I ended up extracting a second tool — Boron — from inside it because the underlying structure deserved its own existence. That is how I tend to think — systems before features, architecture before output.

And when something needs to happen, I do not wait for the ideal conditions. I quit JEE midway through 12th grade because the comparison was obvious. I cold-called a school after the email went unanswered. I called my favourite teacher back after my school lost him. The risks were real. They seemed worth taking.
